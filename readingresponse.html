Reading reflections:

On Photography by Susan Sontag

To me, photography has become a way of capturing a moment that I won’t want to forget and also a way of documenting the events that have happened to me. However, sometimes the picture can be seen as simply taken from the perspective of the photographer, but the viewer won’t get to experience or understand the full meaning of these pictures because the photographer chooses what to include and what is left out. This could manipulate the meaning of the pictures. Whether it’s a selfie or a photograph of scenery, or just making decisions that reflect one’s perspective. One of the easiest ways to capture a photo is to use your phone. I use my phone to capture random moments throughout my day, whether it’s a cup of iced coffee, my reflection on the subway door. To me, taking selfies is one of the most direct ways of presenting your authentic self and how you choose to represent yourself, although some posts on your socials might not be the most original and authentic version of the photos you took, because you can edit the exposure or put a filter on it. These are carefully constructed to present oneself in a certain way or based on how one wants to be perceived by others. Besides selfies, I also enjoy taking photos of the objects that I'm surrounded by, such as sunsets and random advertisement posters on the sidewalk that I find unique or interesting. These still pictures can explain and communicate feelings such as calmness, happiness, etc based on how these pictures are taken, whether it’s the color or the object that is in the frame. We live in a very digital and technology-driven world, where people are able to connect and communicate easily, yet many people still feel very disconnected. I can represent this visually through a picture, literally by taking a picture of a regular day in a cafe. You can see that there are laptops, phones, and notification sounds, and people are literally surrounded by screens, so there really isn't anyone sitting and talking to one another. I could contrast that picture by taking another picture of an empty room or a kindergarten playroom where little kids are playing with toys and enjoying each other's company. Another way to represent the current moment would be to create slides of pictures of NEWS, ads, and social media that we encounter in our everyday life. This could represent the overwhelming amount of information that is being fed to us. This could create a sense of visual overload. The lighting and colors in pictures can convey mood and emotion. Cool lighting can suggest uncertainty and the coldness of the world we're living in today, while warm lighting can suggest nostalgia, community, and maybe hope for something genuine. Using lighting as a tool to communicate instead of using actual words to get my message through. Overall, photography allows me to explore both personal and societal issues. They can communicate ideas immediately. By photographing ordinary moments in certain ways, I can create images that reflect not only what the world looks like, but also how living in the present moment feels.

Your Brain on Chat GPT

In the reading section, I think it was interesting how much using AI can affect the way we learn and think. Since AI has become such a big part of our daily life and schoolwork, it is easy to rely on AI when we have assignments to finish. However, the study has shown that using AI too often might actually make us less engaged in our learning. It was not very surprising to see that the Brain Only group had the strongest brain activity and felt the most ownership over their essays because I think when we write something on our own, we have to think through our ideas and understand what we're saying. I think that AI can be a helpful tool for brainstorming ideas, organizing thoughts, and use it to explain difficult topics, but it should not replace our own thinking. If we just copy what it has given us just so we can finish our work faster, we might not actually remember or understand the information. In the YouTube discussion, I think it was informative how using AI can affect the way we think and learn. Since ChatGPT has become a big part of our lives, especially for the younger generation and students now, we start to depend on it to finish our assignments because it's faster. In the video, they mentioned how people who used ChatGPT experienced a decrease in brain activity compared to those who wrote it manually. One thing that stood out was how they discussed the importance of lifelong learning and keeping our brain active to help prevent cognitive decline and Alzheimer's. I never really thought about how avoiding mental challenges could affect our brains in the long run. I would have to admit that I use AI to help me with my work, but I can see how the constant use of it, it is slowly making me less independent when it comes to learning. They mention how AI can be helpful, and it should be used as a tool rather than a replacement. Instead of just copying and pasting the answer that Chat is giving you, I think we should question and ask follow- up questions on the information that is being provided by AI and also use our judgement. This makes AI more of a useful tool because we are still actively involved in the process. Another topic that they mentioned in the video is that children are now using AI to develop basic critical thinking and social skills. They are also struggling with schoolwork, and interacting with others is an important part of growing up. Them using AI is making things easier; they might miss out on some of the valuable experiences. Overall, I think AI can be helpful, but there needs to be a boundary. Technology should be able to help us learn and grow, but not take away the opportunities to think for ourselves.

Motherhood in Crisis by Laurence Ivil, Alicia Prager and Saidu Bah

The use of illustrations, photographs, text, and animation helps to tell the story in different ways in Chapter 3 of Motherhood in Crisis. It all gives the readers a different experience. I think the illustrations are really important because they can communicate emotions and ideas that may be hard to show through words. Especially in this chapter, where they explored the topic of how limited access there is to healthcare and how the lack of trust in medical facilities can put pregnant women and babies in danger. I noticed that the illustration can show emotions in a different way from photographs. Photography captures real people and places, which makes it feel realistic. It helps the reader to see and understand the environment and the problems that are happening to those women. Illustrations allow the creator to be more expressive through their colors, the shadows, and facial expressions. They can communicate complex emotions. Emotions are important in Kadiau’s story because pregnancy can be a frightening experience when proper medical care is difficult to access. I also think illustrations are useful because not every moment and experience can be captured through photography. A photograph can show what a person looks like, but it can’t really capture the thoughts of the person the way illustration can. They can also help tell stories about sensitive topics when you don’t want to show someone's real face in every scene. In this case, dealing with the loss of her children. In comparison with text, illustration makes the story more engaging and easier to follow. I think reading about the dangers of pregnancy in a place where medical help is scarce and limited can help the reader understand the facts, whereas in illustration it can help the reader visually understand the situation and somewhat make it personal. Instead of just imagining what Kadiatu might be experiencing, the readers can connect the words to the illustration to understand it even further. The combination of the text and the illustration can make difficult topics such as this some can make it easier to understand. Photography and videos are important in their own ways. Photography provides a connection to real people and locations, and videos show us the movement, expressions, and sounds. These forms of media help with storytelling and understanding the story beyond illustration. The combination of these elements can let the readers understand the emotions and the issues surrounding motherhood. Overall, I think the use of illustration in Kadiatu’s story makes the story more powerful because it helps to communicate emotions that words and photography might not be able to fully express. Photography and video show the reality of the situation, while the illustration helps the readers imagine the experiences and feelings of the people involved. Together, these different forms of media make Kadiatu’s story more engaging and encourage the readers to think more deeply about the challenges that pregnant women face when medical and safe healthcare is not easily accessible.
